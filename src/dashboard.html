About Cartly

CARTLY

Support & Resolution Platform

Cartly is a production-grade, event-driven autonomous customer support platform powered by multi-agent AI. Built as part of a capstone research project, it demonstrates how intelligent agents can triage, route, and resolve customer queries at scale — with full observability and a live admin control plane.
